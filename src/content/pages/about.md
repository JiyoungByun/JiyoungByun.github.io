I’m a Ph.D. candidate in Biomedical Engineering at **Johns Hopkins University**, advised by
[Rama Chellappa](https://engineering.jhu.edu/faculty/rama-chellappa/). My research focuses on
reliable reasoning and evaluation for AI systems, with healthcare and scientific discovery as
domains where uncertainty, evidence, and errors have practical consequences.

I study how to improve model answers, determine when they can be trusted, and allocate
computation effectively. This includes hallucination-aware calibration for medical
vision-language models, test-time scaling for clinical decision-making, and evaluation of
multi-agent scientific workflows when reference answers are unavailable.

My work connects method development with hands-on implementation and domain collaboration.
I have collaborated with Microsoft on medical AI reliability, built scientific agents at
Merck, and worked with ophthalmologists at the Wilmer Eye Institute and Aravind Eye Hospital
on smartphone-based cataract screening evaluated prospectively in rural India. These
experiences shape how I choose research problems and design evaluations that reflect the
conditions in which AI will be used.

I’m seeking **Research Scientist** roles where I can advance the capabilities and reliability
of foundation models and agentic systems for health and science.
